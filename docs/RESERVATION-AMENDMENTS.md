# Reservation amendments

Open Travel Platform supports controlled post-booking changes without deleting or rewriting operational history.

## Core rules

- The reservation document represents the current operational state.
- Material trip changes are stored in `travel_reservation_amendments` as immutable before/after history.
- Historical payment transactions remain authoritative for real money movements and are never rewritten by amendments.
- Activities, transport and travel protection remain independent service reservations and can be linked to a trip with `relatedReservationId`.
- Product change/cancellation rules are snapshotted onto each new reservation. Editing a product later does not alter bookings that already exist.
- Legacy reservations without a saved change policy retain the previous permissive behaviour.

## Traveller corrections

Operator/Admin may correct first name, last name and nationality without repricing or inventory movement. A reason is mandatory and the previous value remains in amendment history.

Date of birth and other fields that can affect age, pricing or capacity are not edited through this simple correction flow.

## Departure changes

A non-cancelled trip reservation can move to another future departure when the target has enough capacity and the saved modification policy still allows it.

The server recalculates against the new departure date:

- age at departure;
- pricing band and traveller fare;
- guardian/minor relationships where applicable;
- inventory consumption;
- accommodation dates, allocation and pricing;
- reservation total.

MongoDB applies the move atomically: reserve target trip/room capacity, release previous capacity, update the reservation and write amendment history. Any failure rolls back the complete change.

## Financial result

After a price-changing amendment, the current reservation total is compared with net successfully paid funds.

- **payment due**: current total is higher than net paid;
- **refund review**: net paid is higher than the current total;
- **pending**: a payment/refund is awaiting confirmation;
- **settled**: net paid matches the current total.

No automatic refund or new payment is generated by an amendment. Historical payments/refunds are never edited or deleted.

If saved deposit/installment terms no longer match the amended total, they are treated as outdated and the payment schedule falls back safely to the current full outstanding balance until staff configures new terms.

## Linked services, notifications and deadlines

### Linked services

A service reservation may reference its parent trip reservation through `relatedReservationId`.

The trip detail surfaces linked activities, transport and travel protection for both Operator and customer. Each linked item remains independent, with its own status, service date/time, price, payment history, traveller-data requirements, cancellation policy and inventory movement.

Cancelling a linked service does **not** mutate the trip reservation. When an inventory-backed service is cancelled, its capacity is released in the same MongoDB transaction as the status change. If capacity cannot be released safely, the cancellation rolls back.

### Change-policy snapshot

Trips and services can configure:

- customer self-service cancellation enabled/disabled;
- customer cancellation cutoff in hours before start;
- staff modification cutoff in hours before start;
- staff cancellation cutoff in hours before start;
- customer email after staff-driven changes enabled/disabled.

A blank cutoff means no time limit. No arbitrary business deadline is imposed by the platform.

For trips without a start time, the departure date boundary is used. Timed activities/transports use their service date and start time. Travel protection uses its trip start date.

The policy is copied onto the reservation when it is created. Existing reservations therefore preserve the conditions they were booked under.

### Enforcement

Policy enforcement happens server-side as well as in the UI:

- customer trip/service cancellation is rejected outside the saved customer window;
- staff trip traveller corrections, departure changes and package-supplement changes are rejected outside the staff modification window;
- staff trip/service cancellation is rejected outside the staff cancellation window;
- unavailable actions are hidden from the relevant UI and replaced with a clear explanation.

### Notifications

When enabled by the reservation snapshot:

- staff trip amendments send the customer an updated-reservation email;
- staff trip confirmation/cancellation uses the normal reservation email flow;
- staff service confirmation/cancellation sends a service-specific email.

Customer emails contain the current customer-facing reservation details and link back to My account. Internal amendment reasons are never included.

## Package supplement amendments

Operator can change non-inventory package supplements after a trip reservation has been created.

Supported changes include:

- add or remove a once-per-booking supplement;
- add/remove traveller assignments for a per-traveller supplement;
- remove a historical supplement that is no longer offered;
- reduce traveller assignments on a disabled historical supplement.

### Snapshot pricing rules

Historical commercial facts are protected during the amendment:

- an already contracted supplement keeps its stored `unitPrice` even if the catalogue price has changed;
- a newly added supplement uses the current enabled catalogue price;
- a disabled/missing historical supplement can be kept, reduced or removed;
- a disabled historical per-traveller supplement cannot be expanded to travellers who did not previously have it;
- traveller IDs are resolved against the actual reservation on the server;
- browser-submitted totals are ignored.

The new reservation total is rebuilt from the current trip fare, optional accommodation amount and amended supplement total. The same MongoDB transaction updates the reservation and inserts a `package-addons-change` amendment containing:

- reason and staff actor;
- supplement total before/after;
- reservation total before/after;
- price delta;
- exact `packageAddOnsBefore` and `packageAddOnsAfter` snapshots.

Payment/refund transactions are not changed. The normal payment summary therefore exposes any new balance as `payment_due` or any overpayment as `refund_review`.

## Operator workflow

### Correct traveller

`Operator → Reservations → Reservation → Travellers → Correct traveller details`

Enter the corrected values and a mandatory reason. The history retains before/after values.

### Change departure

`Operator → Reservations → Reservation → Change departure`

Choose an eligible future departure, review recalculated dates/pricing/capacity, provide a reason and save.

### Change package supplements

`Operator → Reservations → Reservation → Package supplements`

Review the contracted snapshot, select the extras/travellers that should remain or be added, provide a mandatory reason and save. Existing contracted unit prices and current catalogue prices are shown separately.

After saving, review **Payments & balance**. No payment or refund is created automatically.

### Review linked services

`Operator → Reservations → Reservation → Linked services`

Open each independent service reservation to confirm or cancel it according to its own saved policy.

## Production checks

1. Configure a trip with customer/staff cutoffs and save it.
2. Create a new trip reservation and confirm the policy is snapshotted.
3. Change the product policy and confirm the existing reservation keeps its original rules.
4. Create a linked activity/transport reservation and confirm it appears in the parent trip for customer and Operator.
5. Cancel an eligible linked service and confirm inventory is released once.
6. Confirm an out-of-window service cancellation is rejected and inventory remains unchanged.
7. Confirm an out-of-window departure/traveller/supplement amendment is rejected.
8. Perform a staff trip amendment while notifications are enabled and confirm the customer receives an updated-reservation email without internal reason text.
9. Book a supplement, change its catalogue price, then amend its traveller assignment and confirm the contracted unit price is preserved.
10. Add a new supplement after booking and confirm it uses the current catalogue price.
11. Disable a previously booked per-traveller supplement and confirm it can be reduced/removed but not expanded to another traveller.
12. Increase the supplement total and confirm payment state becomes additional balance due without a new ledger transaction.
13. Reduce the total below net paid and confirm refund review appears without an automatic refund.
14. Confirm the amendment history preserves exact supplement snapshots before and after.
15. Confirm legacy reservations without a change-policy snapshot continue to operate under the prior behaviour.

## Automated invariant checks

`scripts/reservation-amendment-check.mjs` covers traveller/departure/financial/change-policy behaviour.

`scripts/package-addon-amendment-check.mjs` covers:

- contracted price preservation;
- current catalogue pricing for newly added extras;
- disabled historical supplement safeguards;
- traveller validation;
- removal/reduction behaviour;
- deterministic snapshot comparison.

Standard CI runs both invariant checks before TypeScript/build validation.
